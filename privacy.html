TopLink Call was built on one principle: your communication belongs to you, not to us.

TopLink Call is a Zero Privacy Violation System

We do not collect, store, process, share, or sell any personal data — ever.

Your phone number and country code are used only at the moment of TopLink ID generation and are immediately discarded. They are never written to any database, log file, or storage system.

2. Your TopLink Call ID

Your TopLink ID (e.g. TLC-00001-NG) is generated mathematically from your phone number and country code at the moment of registration. Once generated:

  • The phone number used to generate it is immediately discarded
  • Your TopLink ID is your permanent identity on the platform
  • It cannot be transferred to a different phone number
  • If you change devices, entering the same phone number and country regenerates the same ID
  • Your ID is what others use to call you — your phone number is never revealed

"Your TopLink ID is yours permanently. It is mathematically derived — not assigned from a database — so it is always recoverable by you alone."

3. Calls, Messages and Media

All communication through TopLink Call is transient:

  • Voice and video calls — transmitted in real time and not recorded or stored by us under any circumstances
  • Text messages, voice notes and video clips — Voice notes, video clips and text messages are end-to-end encrypted and held temporarily on our servers only until delivered or for a maximum of 7 days, then permanently deleted. Our servers cannot read or play your messages. Once delivered, they are stored only on the devices of the sender and recipient.
  • Call recordings — if you choose to record a call, it is saved to your device only. We have no access to it
  • Meeting notes — stored on your device only. We do not have access to your notes

4. What We Monitor

To keep the service running reliably, we monitor only aggregate, anonymous statistics:

  • Number of active users at any given time (count only, no identities)
  • Number of calls in progress (count only, no content)
  • App health and server uptime
  • Error rates (technical only, no personal data)

These statistics contain no personal identifiers. We cannot tell who is making a call, only that calls are happening.

5. Device Security

TopLink Call does not impose or manage security on your device. You are responsible for securing your own device and your TopLink ID using whatever method your device offers — Face ID, fingerprint, PIN, or password.

We do not store your security credentials and cannot help you recover access if you lose your device without a backup of your TopLink ID.

"Your security is your responsibility. We provide the communication infrastructure. You protect your access to it."

7. Legal Requests and Law Enforcement

⚖️ Our position on legal requests:

TopLink Call cannot provide what it does not have or cannot read. Calls are never recorded. Messages are end-to-end encrypted: the encrypted copies we hold briefly for delivery cannot be read or played by us, and are permanently deleted once delivered or after 7 days. We therefore have no call or message content to hand over in response to any legal request, court order, or law enforcement inquiry — regardless of jurisdiction.

TopLink Call advises and encourages all users to use this service within the laws and constitution of their country. We are not liable or accountable for how users choose to use this service, and we cannot provide any information or evidence regarding any specific user or communication.

8. User Responsibility

By using TopLink Call, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • You are solely responsible for how you use this service
  • You will comply with the laws of your country
  • You are responsible for the security of your own device and TopLink ID
  • Content you share via TopLink Call is your responsibility
  • TopLink Call cannot be held liable for how you use the communication infrastructure we provide

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify users through the app. Our core commitment — storing nothing about you — will never change. That is the foundation TopLink Call was built on.
